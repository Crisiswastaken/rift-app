# RIFT

**Play your phone games with your body.**

Put your phone on a stand, show the game on your TV if you like, and jump, duck, punch, swing and slice. RIFT watches you
through the camera (or reads a second phone you hold) and taps the game for you. Everything runs on your phones. Nothing
is sent anywhere.

> RIFT is in **beta**. It works well with the games below, and we'd love to hear how it goes for you.
> Found a problem? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose).

## Download

Get the latest APK from **[Releases](../../releases/latest)** and open it on your Android phone.
Android will ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or files app. That's normal for apps outside the Play Store.

You need Android 11 or newer.

## Games in this beta

| Game | Play with | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Subway Surfers | Camera | Jump, duck, and lean left or right to switch lanes |
| Real Boxing 2 | Camera | Punch with either hand, cover your face to block |
| World Cricket Championship 3 | A second phone as your bat, or your hand | Swing like a bat |
| Fruit Ninja | A second phone as your blade, or your hand | Point and slice |
| Beat Swiper | Two extra phones, one in each hand | Swing in the direction of each arrow |

Install the game from the Play Store first. RIFT finds it by itself. More games are coming.

## Getting started

1. Install RIFT and the game you want to play.
2. Open RIFT and pick **Play games on this phone**.
3. Allow the camera and turn on RIFT in Accessibility settings. That's how RIFT taps the game for you. The app shows you
   exactly where to find it.
4. Stand the phone up 2 to 3 metres away with the camera facing you. A tripod, a shelf or a pile of books all work.
5. Open the game in RIFT and do the short calibration it asks for. Then tap **Play**.

In the game, a light at the top tells you how things are going: **green** means RIFT can see you, **amber** means adjust
something (like "Step back"), **red** means something needs fixing first.

### Using a second phone as a controller

For the cricket bat, the Fruit Ninja blade or the Beat Swiper sabers, install RIFT on the other phone too and pick
**Use this phone as a controller**. Put both phones on the same Wi-Fi, then type the 4-digit code shown on the game
phone. Next time they reconnect by themselves.

## Tips

- **Light matters.** A bright room helps a lot. Don't stand with a bright window behind you.
- **Show it on a TV** with a USB-C to HDMI cable or screen casting, so you can see the game from where you stand.
- **Keep RIFT running.** Set RIFT's battery use to Unrestricted. On some phones (vivo, iQOO, Xiaomi, OPPO) also turn on
  auto-start for RIFT.
- **Done playing?** Swipe RIFT away from your recent apps, or tap Stop in its notification. It also stops by itself after
  a few minutes without a game.

## Privacy

The camera picture is processed on your phone and never leaves it. RIFT has no account and no servers. The controller
phone talks to the game phone directly over your own Wi-Fi.

## Reporting a problem

[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) and tell us your phone model, the game, and what happened. A short video helps a
